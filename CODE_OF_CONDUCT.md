# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Explain the why behind a request or a review comment.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other space of this
project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the
repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, marking it as a
conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads or block participants who break these rules.
